## 🔥 Core Features at a Glance

### 1. 🚀 1-Click Cross-Lister (Save $360–$720/Year)
Port your active inventory between marketplaces in seconds. Titles, tags, and formatted descriptions automatically adapt to destination constraints (e.g. 13 tags under 20 chars for Etsy, 80 chars for eBay, 5 hashtags for Depop). The floating **QuickBar** carries over up to 8 listing photos and fills forms in 1 click.

### 2. 📱 Wire-Free Mobile Photo Sync (Phone-to-Chrome)
Resellers spend up to 40% of their prep time emailing photos to themselves or fighting cloud sync delays. With QuickSnipe:
1. Open the **Tools ➔ Photo Sync** tab in your side panel.
2. Scan the secure pairing QR code with your iPhone or Android camera.
3. Snap items at your photo table or thrift haul — photos beam directly to your PC in real time, ready to save to disk or send straight into AI Studio!

### 3. 🎯 Sold Comp Oracle & Outlier Removal (1.5x IQR)
Asking prices are fantasies; only **sold comps** pay the bills.
* **1.5x IQR Filtering:** Automatically discards junk, for-parts listings, and bulk lots to protect your margins.
* **0.88x Best Offer Factor:** Adjusts accepted strikethrough offers to reflect true transaction cash values.
* **Corridor Pricing:** Delivers **Quick Sale (25th percentile)**, **Market Median (50th percentile)**, and **Top Dollar (75th percentile)** targets.

### 4. ⚖️ USPS Ground Advantage "Ounce Trap" Advisor
USPS postage jumps steeply at the 16.0 oz boundary (from ~$6.20 to $8.40+). QuickSnipe adds your packaging weight, calculates total ounces, warns you when heavy boxes risk pushing you over the cliff, and suggests lighter poly mailers so you stop burning profit at the post office.

### 5. 📡 Deal Radar — Saved Searches That Hunt For You
Save your buying criteria once: maximum buy price, expected resale target, profit floor, and negative keywords. QuickSnipe monitors marketplaces in the background, pinging you via desktop notifications and toolbar badges when underpriced inventory drops. Watch competitor shops for restocks and price drops.

### 6. 🤝 Buyer Offer Negotiator & Counteroffer Matrix
Stop guessing when lowballed. Input buyer offers on eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, or Depop to see your exact net cash after commission tiers and shipping splits, plus a 3-tier counteroffer matrix with a guaranteed profit floor.

### 7. 📦 Flip Tracker & 1-Click Schedule C Tax Rollup
Track every item from **Sourced ➔ Listed ➔ Sold**. View live KPIs (Invested Capital, Net Profit, ROI %), monitor aging inventory buckets (>90-day dead stock), and export 1-click accountant-ready IRS Schedule C CSV spreadsheets at tax time.

## 🛡️ Privacy, Security & Technical Architecture

QuickSnipe is engineered as a **local-first, 100% client-side Chrome Side Panel application**:
* **Zero Middleman Servers:** Listing drafts, flip ledgers, and inventory data remain on your local machine (`chrome.storage.local`).
* **Passive Browser Automation:** Interacts with listing forms using standard DOM typing and input events. Never uses bot crawlers, headless automation, or account logins.
* **Optional BYOK Cloud Boost:** Connect your personal API key (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, Groq, Ollama) in Settings anytime. Keys are stored encrypted in your local browser storage and never touch third-party servers.
