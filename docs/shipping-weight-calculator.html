How Resellers Avoid the USPS 16 oz Cliff (Margin Protection)

📦

Switch from Box to Polymailer

A small cardboard box weighs 3.5 to 5.0 ounces. A tear-proof polymailer weighs only 0.4 ounces. For jeans, hoodies, and soft goods, switching to a polymailer routinely saves 3+ ounces and prevents the rate spike.

✂️

Trim Box Flaps & Excess Tape

If your package weighs 16.1 or 16.2 ounces, you are paying an extra $4.00+ for just two tenths of an ounce. Trimming excess cardboard flaps and using one strip of quality tape rather than five layers will drop you back into the safe tier.

⚖️

Calibrate Your Digital Scale

Cheap postal scales drift over time. If your scale reads 15.8 oz but the USPS automated sorting conveyor reads 16.05 oz, you will receive an automated shipping adjustment fee on eBay or Etsy weeks later.
