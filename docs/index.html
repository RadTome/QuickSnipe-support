How to send photos from your phone to your PC with QuickSnipe

  1. Open QuickSnipe on your computer and click Snap on Phone. A QR code appears.
  2. Point your phone's camera at the code and tap the link. It opens in Safari or Chrome, so there's nothing to install.
  3. Take photos or pick them from your gallery. You can send up to 10 at once.
  4. Watch them appear on your computer, where QuickSnipe can start writing your listing right away.
