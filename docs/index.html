Privacy, Permissions & How BYOK Works

What stays local, what goes to AI, and why your accounts stay safe

Clear answers to the two questions every reseller asks before installing an extension: what happens to my photos/keys, and will this ever flag my seller account?

1 · Stays on Your Device

100% Local Browser Storage

QuickSnipe runs inside Chrome with zero developer user accounts, zero analytics SDKs, and bundled local fonts (zero remote CDN calls).

  • Flip CRM, COGS & Profits: Saved strictly in chrome.storage.local — never synced to any server.
  • Swipes, Deal Radar & Keys: Saved locally; settings backups mask API keys and strip license keys.
  • Optional Phone QR Sync: Ephemeral relay purges undelivered photos automatically in 60s.
2 · Direct BYOK or Offline AI

How AI & BYOK Work in Practice

QuickSnipe works right out of the box with no API key. If you connect your own BYOK key (Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, Groq, DeepSeek, OpenRouter):

  • Direct HTTPS Calls: Chrome sends only the active item's text or selected photo straight to that provider — never via QuickSnipe servers.
  • Prompt-Injection Shield: Scraped text is isolated inside <untrusted_marketplace_data> delimiters.
  • 100% Offline Option: Use Chrome Built-in AI (Gemini Nano) or local Ollama with zero cloud calls.
3 · Seller Account Safety

Zero-Permission Install & Human Control

Marketplace accounts stay safe because QuickSnipe behaves like a human assistant inside your own open tab:

  • v1.5.0 Opt-In Permissions: Asks for zero marketplace site permissions at install (only extensionpay.com for licensing). Enable & revoke per platform anytime.
  • No Bots or Logins: Never sees passwords, cookies, or payment details; uses standard DOM input events.
  • You Click Publish: Every listing draft and price is reviewed by you before publishing.
